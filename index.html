💬 Language Bank — Use these expressions

❓ Asking for opinions

  • What do you think?
  • How about you?
  • What's your opinion?

🧠 Giving opinions

  • I think…
  • In my opinion…
  • For me…

💡 Making suggestions

  • You should…
  • Why don't you…?
  • How about…?

👍 Agreeing / Disagreeing

  • I agree. / That's a good idea.
  • I don't agree.
  • I see your point, but…
